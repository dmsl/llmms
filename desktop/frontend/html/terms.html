Terms of Service

The rules and commitments behind ChatUCY.

01
Introduction

Welcome to ChatUCY, a project developed by the University of Cyprus to provide accessible AI-powered chat services using open-source models. Our platform is built for educational, research, and non-commercial use, giving individuals access to AI models without cost barriers.

02
Use of Services

By using ChatUCY, you agree to use our services responsibly and for lawful purposes. Our services are offered "as-is" without any warranty or guarantee.

03
Non-Storage of Data

ChatUCY values your privacy and does not store your personal data or chat history. Once a session ends, the data is cleared and cannot be retrieved.

04
Open-Source Models

The platform uses open-source AI models to promote accessibility and inclusivity for all users, especially those who cannot afford paid alternatives.

05
Limitations

ChatUCY is a research-oriented platform and may not be suitable for handling sensitive or critical information. The university does not assume responsibility for the accuracy of responses generated by the AI.
