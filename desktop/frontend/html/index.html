How It Works

ChatUCY

ChatUCY works by directly forwarding your query to a large language model for processing – all without storing any data. This ensures your privacy while providing fast and intelligent responses. Additionally, all chat history is saved on your device, keeping your interactions secure and private.

About

ChatUCY is an AI chatbot platform developed to assist users across various tasks, enabling smooth and natural interactions. Whether you're seeking information, assistance, or simply engaging in a conversation, ChatUCY is your free-to-use platform.
