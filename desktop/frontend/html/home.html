An AI-powered assistant for seamless and engaging conversations, built at the University of Cyprus.

Features

Engage in dynamic, context-aware, and intelligent interactions powered by state-of-the-art open-source AI models.

02
How It Works
Zero-Store Direct Forwarding

Direct Query Forwarding

ChatUCY works by directly forwarding your query to a large language model for processing — without storing any data. This ensures your privacy while providing fast and intelligent responses. All chat history is saved only on your device.

LLM-MS additionally routes each query to the most suitable model using multi-armed bandit and overperformer-underperformer selection algorithms.
03
About

ChatUCY is an AI chatbot platform developed to assist users across various tasks, enabling smooth and natural interactions. Whether you're seeking information, assistance, or simply engaging in a conversation, ChatUCY is your free-to-use platform.

Built by the Data Management Systems Laboratory (DMSL) at the University of Cyprus, ChatUCY leverages open-source AI models to promote accessibility and inclusivity for all users.

Research-grade AI, accessible to everyone.
