Privacy Policy

Introduction

At ChatUCY, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information during your use of our platform.

Data Collection

ChatUCY does not collect or store your personal data or chat history. All interactions occur in real-time and are not logged or saved by the system.

Use of Open-Source Models

Our platform uses open-source AI models to provide chat functionality. These models run on our university's infrastructure and are not shared with third-party providers.

Security

Although we do not store user data, our platform implements industry-standard security measures to ensure a safe browsing experience.

Your Rights

As we do not retain any user data, you are assured of complete privacy during your session. If you have any concerns or questions, please contact us at dmsl@cs.ucy.ac.cy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at dmsl@cs.ucy.ac.cy.
